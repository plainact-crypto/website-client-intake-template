Target query: web design discovery questions

Business questions

  • What does the business sell?
  • Who is the ideal customer?
  • What makes the business different?
  • What problem should the website solve?

Project questions

  • Why now?
  • What is not working on the current site?
  • What pages are needed?
  • What functionality is required?
  • What is the deadline?

Risk questions

  • Is content ready?
  • Who approves design?
  • Are logins available?
  • Is the deadline flexible?
  • Are there legal or compliance requirements?

Budget and fit questions

  • What is the expected investment range?
  • Is maintenance needed?
  • Is copywriting included?
  • Is branding needed?
  • Is this project a good fit for your process?
